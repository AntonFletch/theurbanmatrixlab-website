Good to know

Straightforward terms.

Does the setup payment automatically subscribe me?

No. Website setup is a one-time purchase. You only enroll in ongoing monthly care through a separate authorization.

When do the first 30 days of included care begin?

On the documented day the completed customer website actually goes live—not when the setup fee is paid.

When are website checkups?

Basic and Business receive one scheduled checkup each week. Continuity receives scheduled checkups on Monday and Friday only. The exact day for Basic or Business will be confirmed in the service agreement.

Is a domain included?

Domain connection is included when within the package's standard scope. Purchasing the domain and external hosting or premium tools are separate expenses.

Can I get a store, booking system or customer portal?

These may be quoted as custom work. They are not automatically included in the advertised packages.

Do you promise instant response or continuous monitoring?

No. You can report an issue at any time once the support channel is configured. Checkups occur on the scheduled days; no 24/7 automated monitoring or instant repair is promised.

Can I cancel monthly care?

Yes. Monthly care may be canceled anytime after separate enrollment. The service agreement will explain the effective billing date and how to cancel.
