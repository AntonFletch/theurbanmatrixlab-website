Website Ownership & Handoff Terms

MX CONTINUITY | Incorporated into the Customer Service Agreement | Version 1.0 | November 2, 2026

1. Client-owned assets

The Customer retains ownership of the business name, customer-provided text and photos, trademarks, client records, and domain names legally registered in its name. MX recommends that a Customer buy and maintain a domain through an account under the Customer's control. A domain or platform license supplied by a third party is subject to that third party's separate terms; purchasing service from MX does not itself transfer registration or vendor ownership.

2. Assignment of original commissioned work

Upon receipt of the full agreed setup fee, MX assigns to Customer all rights it owns under U.S. copyright law in the original, custom text, graphics, page designs, and code specifically created as paid deliverables for that Customer in its Project Order, to the extent transferable. This is an express written assignment by MX through the signed Service Agreement and incorporated ownership terms; it is not a claim that every third-party component is owned by Customer. Customer's license to otherwise available pre-existing materials is addressed below.

3. Materials MX and others keep

MX keeps ownership of pre-existing and reusable templates, generic components, libraries, scripts, methods, design systems, internal administrative systems, operational know-how, and its trade names, logos, and company symbols. MX gives Customer a nonexclusive, worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free license to use and modify any MX-owned reusable elements actually included in the paid delivered site, solely as part of operating, reproducing, modifying, or migrating that Customer's own website. MX may reuse those generic elements for others. Third-party software, fonts, stock images, themes, APIs, and hosting remain governed by their own licenses, which MX will identify at handoff when reasonably practicable.

4. Access, source, and portability

Within ten business days after completed payment and go-live, or within ten business days after a properly requested paid handoff following termination, MX will provide reasonably available: site URL, hosting and domain provider names; login/administrator roles that Customer is entitled to hold; original paid custom source files or platform export where supported; major dependencies; configured form destinations; and a short handoff note. Passwords and API keys are to be delivered through a reasonably secure channel. Third-party platforms can restrict exportability; MX will explain such restrictions in the Project Order or at handoff, not falsely promise unrestricted source export.

5. Cancellation does not transfer or delete ownership

Stopping care does not strip Customer of fully paid original work or customer-owned domains. Customer remains responsible for hosting, domain renewal, licenses, backups, and subsequent maintenance. MX will not intentionally delete paid deliverables as retaliation for cancellation. MX may remove its access after the agreed handoff and service termination.

6. Customer data and confidential access

Customer owns business and customer records provided to MX, subject to applicable law and individual data rights. MX will export or return reasonably available Customer data on a lawful written request and remove its access after handoff. MX may retain limited invoice, agreement, security and compliance records as required by law, with backup remnants expiring under the applicable retention schedule. The parties will separately agree on special data-migration or restoration work requiring substantial effort.

7. Completion checklist

Customer received: □ Domain registration owner confirmed □ Host/platform and billing owner confirmed □ Administrator access □ Paid custom files or export instructions □ Third-party license list where applicable □ Care end/renewal explanation □ Dated go-live confirmation.
