6. Approval, changes and governing documents

This Change Order supplements the existing Project Order and Website Design & Care Service Agreement. The original agreement, Refund Policy, Cancellation Policy, confidentiality, warranty, security, cancellation and handoff terms continue to apply. If there is a conflict, this Change Order controls only the specifically identified changed work, schedule and approved charges; otherwise the original contract prevails.

Under MX's standard authorization and billing procedure, additional billable work is approved and scheduled only after Customer and an authorized MX representative sign this completed Change Order. The parties may sign on paper or, if both agree to transact electronically, use an electronic signature method that reasonably identifies the signer and records their intent. Each party must receive an accurate, accessible and printable copy of the executed version and any listed attachments. An inquiry, unsigned draft or verbal estimate alone does not authorize a new charge. This procedure does not limit any modifications or rights that applicable law independently recognizes, including Montana contract law. Further billable changes must be documented and accepted through the same procedure.

Change-specific cancellation and refunds: If a signed Change Order is canceled, any refund or credit is calculated using the reasonable, documented amount of change-order work actually completed, the expressly agreed milestones and any separately approved nonrefundable external expenses, subject to the Refund Policy and applicable law. The original setup-fee stage percentages are not automatically applied to a separate Change Order unless this Change Order specifically adopts them. An unearned prepayment or agreed credit will be returned or applied as documented. Cancellation of a Change Order alone does not cancel the original project or an independently authorized monthly-care subscription.
