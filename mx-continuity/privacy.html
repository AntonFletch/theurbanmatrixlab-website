Privacy Policy

MX CONTINUITY | Public website privacy notice | Last updated November 2, 2026

MX Continuity ("MX," "we," "us") is a website design and optional care service intended to be operated by The Urban Matrix Lab LLC. Before publishing this notice, verify that the contracting entity and assumed-business-name filing match the actual business registration. This notice covers MX's own marketing website, customer inquiries, project onboarding, service communications, and customer-support records. It does not automatically serve as the privacy policy for websites built for customers; those customers need notices specific to their own practices.

1. Personal information we collect

When you contact us or order a service, we may collect your name, business name, email, phone number, website/domain information, messages, requested services, files you choose to send, project approvals, invoices, and support communications. For a customer account, we may process account identifiers and authentication records through our account provider; we do not ask you to send passwords in an ordinary email. Our website, hosting platform, and security systems may process technical data such as IP address, browser/device type, dates, pages accessed, and logs needed to operate and protect the service.

Payments are processed by a designated third-party payment provider when checkout is enabled. We receive transaction status, amount, and limited billing information necessary for records and support; we do not intend to receive or store complete card numbers or card security codes on our own web pages.

2. How we use the information

We use information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, verify and deliver project work, operate agreed customer accounts and support, process and reconcile authorized payments, provide requested service notices, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain site security, enforce our agreements, and meet tax, bookkeeping, or legal requirements. We do not use business inquiry information for unrelated marketing without any legally required permission; you can opt out of promotional email.

3. Why and with whom information is shared

We share only data reasonably necessary with service providers such as hosting/domain vendors, managed databases and authentication systems, email/communications services, and a payment processor such as Stripe when those systems are activated. Providers may process information in other U.S. states or countries under their own safeguards and policies. We may also share information if required by law, to protect rights and security, or as part of a lawful business transfer subject to suitable protections. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising under this policy. Adding advertising pixels, profiling, or new analytics would require updating this notice and implementing any required consent or opt-out controls before activation.

4. Cookies and website technology

Our intended public website uses only cookies or similar technologies necessary for security, session/login functions, and the operation of a requested service. We do not intend to activate third-party advertising trackers. If optional analytics or nonessential cookies are added, we will update the policy and any required choice mechanism first. Customer websites may use different tools and require their own notices.

5. Data retention

We aim to retain general inquiries for up to 24 months after the last meaningful interaction; routine support tickets for up to 24 months after the associated service closes; technical security logs generally up to 90 days; and signed agreements, invoices, billing records, and necessary tax documents for up to seven years or the legally required period. Some information may be retained longer when reasonably necessary to resolve disputes, investigate abuse, preserve legal evidence, or meet law. Vendor backups can persist temporarily under provider-controlled cycles. We will delete or de-identify other data when no longer reasonably needed.

6. Security and breach response

We use reasonable access controls, authentication practices, limited staff access, and protected services where available. No service is completely secure. If legally covered personal information is compromised, we will investigate and provide legally required notices, including disclosures required by Montana law. Please do not include payment-card details, government identifiers, or sensitive personal records in an ordinary inquiry form.

7. Your privacy choices

You may write to TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you, subject to verification and any legal exceptions. You may also ask for a copy of information or unsubscribe from promotional communications. Where the Montana Consumer Data Privacy Act or another applicable law provides additional rights to qualifying individuals, we will honor those rights, including eligible opt-out rights, through a reasonable process. We do not claim that every statute applies to every visitor or every small business. We will not discriminate unlawfully for an applicable privacy-rights request.

8. Children and third-party links

Our services are aimed at adults acting for a business. They are not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly solicit their information. If you believe a child submitted information, contact us for review. Our site may link to external providers whose privacy practices are controlled by their own notices.

9. Updates and contact

We will post a revised date if the notice materially changes and provide additional notice and choice when legally required. Email privacy requests and questions to TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com (temporary business contact; an MX-specific address should replace it once verified). The legal operator is The Urban Matrix Lab LLC.

Questions about these terms? Email TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com. Service obligations are confirmed through an accepted written project order.
