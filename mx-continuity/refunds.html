Refund Policy

MX CONTINUITY | Public customer policy | Effective November 9, 2026

1. Overview

MX Continuity offers fixed-scope website creation at $89 (Basic), $199 (Business), or $400 (Continuity). Monthly care, if separately authorized, costs $25, $49, or $89 per month, respectively. This policy distinguishes setup work from recurring care. Applicable mandatory consumer rights always prevail over these contract terms.

Optional Domain & Hosting Setup: The separate $99 one-time coordination fee is charged only after written authorization. Outside domain, hosting and renewal fees are not included and normally are paid by the Customer directly to providers. If canceled, MX retains only documented authorized work actually completed, subject to applicable law.

2. Before setup work begins

If the Customer cancels an accepted project before MX has started any billable work and before any specifically authorized third-party purchase, MX will return the setup payment in full. MX will not deduct undisclosed processing or administration fees. If an authorized, nonrefundable third-party expense was already incurred, we will provide proof and refund the remaining unearned portion to the extent legally permitted.

3. After setup work starts but before launch

A Customer may cancel by emailing TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com. MX will provide a brief, documented accounting of completed work, delivered materials, and any preapproved third-party expenses. For fixed-fee projects, the setup value is allocated as follows only for work actually performed:

Work stage Maximum allocation of setup fee
Discovery, content organization and agreed project plan 20%
Design and construction of the scoped pages/functionality 50%
Testing, final corrections, domain/host launch work and handoff 30%

MX will retain only the reasonable, documented portion of the applicable stages completed, not automatically the full allocation for a partially completed stage, plus authorized nonrefundable third-party purchases. Any unused balance will be refunded. This accounting may never exceed the agreed setup price plus separately approved external expenses. Customer receives completed paid-for custom deliverables as permitted under the Ownership & Handoff Terms.

4. Change Orders and additional work

If a separate Change Order & Additional Work Authorization is accepted, canceling that extra work does not automatically cancel the original Project Order or a separately authorized care plan. A change-only refund or credit is calculated from the signed change scope, completed and documented work, payment milestones, and preapproved nonrefundable third-party expenses. The 20%/50%/30% allocation above describes the original setup project and is not automatically applied to standalone change-order fees unless the parties expressly agree to it in the signed Change Order. MX will return any unearned change-order prepayment or authorized credit, subject to applicable law and any properly disclosed, documented expenses.

5. After approval and launch

Once Customer has reviewed and accepted the substantially conforming completed website and the site is launched, setup fees normally compensate delivered custom work and are not refunded for a change of mind. If the site materially fails to match the signed Project Order, notify MX in writing with a description. MX will have a reasonable opportunity to correct the nonconformity without another setup charge. Where MX cannot remedy a substantiated material failure, the parties will arrange an appropriate partial or full refund for undelivered or defective work as required by law and the agreement.

6. Optional monthly care charges

Monthly charges are never permitted merely because a setup payment was made or 30 included-care days have passed. A subscription requires separate express authorization. Cancellation stops future care charges and generally leaves already-paid current service accessible through the paid billing period. There is no automatic cash refund for a partially used month simply because Customer cancels; however, MX will refund duplicate or unauthorized charges, correct billing errors, and provide legally required or appropriate refunds for material service failures. If a recurring care charge was taken without proper consent, MX will promptly investigate and reverse any invalid charge.

7. How to request a refund

Email TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com with the customer business name, any available order reference, requested remedy, and a safe reply address. An order number is not required if you can otherwise identify the transaction. Do not send full card numbers. MX will acknowledge the request within two business days and issue a reasoned written decision with itemized accounting within ten business days after receipt of the request. MX may request reasonable verification without using that request to extend the written decision deadline; a faster legal or payment-network deadline controls. The decision will identify the refund calculation, any documented completed work or preapproved external expense retained, and the amount approved for return. MX will initiate an approved refund to the original payment method within ten business days after approval, or sooner when legally required; the bank or card provider may take additional time to post it. A refund request does not require Customer to waive statutory rights or withdraw a lawful payment dispute. Cancellation and refunds are distinct: cancellation stops future work or renewals as described in the Cancellation Policy, while this policy determines any money owed back. Applicable mandatory remedies remain available.

Questions about these terms? Email TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com. Service obligations are confirmed through an accepted written project order.
