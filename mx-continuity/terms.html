Website Design & Care Service Agreement

MX CONTINUITY | Customer agreement | Version 1.0 | Prepared November 2, 2026

Provider: The Urban Matrix Lab LLC, offering services under the MX Continuity brand (the Provider or MX).
Customer: The individual or legal entity identified on the accepted project order (the Customer).
Contact: TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com.

1. Agreement and acceptance

This agreement governs MX's website-design, launch, and optional maintenance services. An individual project becomes binding only when MX and Customer accept a written Project Order identifying the package, deliverables, price, timetable, domain/hosting responsibilities, and any separately quoted expenses. Electronic signatures and documented online assent may be used. If an order changes this agreement, the express change must be signed or affirmatively accepted by both sides. No customer payment is due until an order is accepted and the checkout process is enabled.

2. Fixed-scope website packages

Plan One-time setup Pages included Optional care Scheduled checkups
Basic $89 One standard page $25/month Once each week
Business $199 Up to five standard pages $49/month Once each week
Continuity $400 Up to eight standard pages $89/month Monday and Friday

A standard page is a responsive, brochure-style page using agreed business text, customer-supplied or properly licensed imagery, ordinary navigation, and agreed contact details. The written Project Order controls whether a standard inquiry form, basic acknowledgment, and other listed features are included; none is promised unless the order specifies it. MX will provide a mobile-responsive layout and conduct reasonable browser and link checks before proposed launch.

Excluded unless separately quoted and approved: domain registration, third-party hosting and platform subscriptions, paid applications, ecommerce or customer payment systems, custom user accounts/portals, bespoke backend workflows, complex integrations, copywriting beyond agreed supplied content, SEO rank guarantees, accessibility certification, ongoing unlimited changes, and any regulated professional services.

3. Project process and customer responsibilities

Customer supplies accurate business information, permitted text/images/logos, timely consolidated feedback, required access to domain/hosting accounts, and any business-specific legal disclosures. Customer warrants it has permission to use submitted materials and is responsible for the legality of its products, claims, privacy notices, and customer-facing practices. MX may decline unlawful or infringing content.

MX provides a preview and one consolidated, in-scope revision round as part of setup. A revision round means one collected set of reasonable changes consistent with the approved order; additional pages, functionality, rebranding, or changes after approval require a separate written quote. Client feedback is requested within ten business days of preview; delays may move the timeline. Both parties will document material changes and costs before work proceeds.

4. Setup payment and third-party expenses

The chosen one-time setup fee is payable in the amount and at the milestone specified in the signed Project Order. MX will not silently enroll Customer in recurring care. Domain, hosting, software, licensing, third-party fees, and taxes if applicable are separate unless expressly included in the written order. MX will not buy nonrefundable third-party products on Customer's behalf without advance written authorization of the price and refund consequences. Payment-card details are collected by the designated compliant payment provider, not requested by email.

5. Approval, launch, and included care

Go-live occurs only when Customer approves the deliverable in writing, the chosen hosting/domain is connected, and MX provides a dated launch confirmation. The launch confirmation identifies the website address and starts 30 consecutive days of included care at no additional monthly care charge. Setup payment alone does not start the 30-day period.

The included care follows the selected package's checkup cadence. A scheduled checkup includes reasonable examination of site reachability, key links or contact-form function where MX has access, routine maintenance needs on the managed platform, and recording significant issues and actions. When a correction is outside the original scope or requires a paid external service, MX will seek written authorization first. Checkups are not continuous uptime monitoring, an emergency-response guarantee, 24/7 support, or a promise to prevent every outage or security incident.

6. Optional monthly website care

Monthly care is separate and opt-in. No automatic subscription begins from a setup payment or from the expiration of included care. MX may offer recurring care after launch with an independent, unchecked authorization that states the specific amount ($25, $49, or $89), frequency, first charge date, payment processor, cancellation method, and any separately authorized platform costs. If Customer opts in during the included 30 days, the first monthly care charge may not occur before day 31 following go-live; if Customer does not opt in, MX stops care at the end of the included period without billing. Future charges recur monthly until cancelled, subject to lawful notice of price changes and fresh agreement to any changed price.

The care plan consists of the scheduled reviews shown in Section 2, reasonable in-scope checks and maintenance notes, and communication of significant findings. It does not provide unlimited edits, redesigns, new features, hosting charges, third-party license payments, or guaranteed continuous availability. Those require written quotes. A customer may terminate monthly care under the Cancellation Policy without a cancellation fee.

7. Intellectual property and account ownership

Customer keeps ownership of materials it provides and should register its own domain in an account it controls. After MX receives the full agreed setup payment, MX assigns to Customer the copyright it owns in original custom deliverables created specifically for that Customer under the Project Order, subject to the separate Website Ownership & Handoff Terms incorporated into this agreement. MX retains its pre-existing templates, general libraries, development methods, tooling, internal systems, MX/UMX identity assets, and third-party-licensed components. MX grants the operating licenses described in the ownership terms. Where MX performs maintenance, Customer gives MX limited access and permission solely to carry out the agreed work.

8. Domain, hosting, security, and continuity

Customer bears recurring domain/hosting costs and must keep necessary third-party accounts current unless a written order clearly assigns these duties to MX. MX will explain material platform limitations and provide available credentials or exportable custom files at handoff. MX uses reasonable security controls for managed access, but no website, hosting platform, or network can be warranted perfectly secure. Each party must safeguard its credentials and promptly notify the other about suspected compromise affecting the service. MX will use applicable data-breach notification procedures when legally required.

9. Changes, suspension, and termination

Either party may terminate ongoing monthly care as stated in the Cancellation Policy. Either may terminate an uncompleted build for a material breach not remedied within ten business days after written notice, except breaches requiring immediate action to prevent illegality or security harm. A Customer may also cancel an uncompleted build for convenience under the Refund Policy; MX will account for work actually completed and authorized third-party expenses. MX will not retain Customer's fully paid original deliverables or Customer-owned domain as leverage over an unrelated care subscription. Transfer requests are addressed under the Ownership & Handoff Terms.

10. Confidentiality and data

Each party will use nonpublic customer/business information only to deliver the service, limit access to authorized personnel and providers, and apply reasonable safeguards. These duties do not cover publicly available information, lawfully obtained information, or disclosures legally required after reasonable notice when permitted. The Privacy Policy covers MX's own website and inquiry operations. If MX processes personal data on behalf of Customer through Customer's website, Customer remains responsible for its website privacy notice and legally required permissions, and the parties will execute additional data-processing terms when needed.

11. Warranty, liability, and disputes

MX will perform work with reasonable care consistent with the accepted Project Order. Customer must report material in-scope defects discovered during the included 30 days; MX will use reasonable efforts to correct them without an additional setup charge. No guarantee is given regarding revenue, leads, search ranking, uninterrupted service, or third-party uptime. Except where prohibited by law, neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential damages; MX's aggregate contractual liability will not exceed the amount Customer paid MX for the affected service during the preceding 12 months. This limit does not excuse fraud, willful misconduct, payment obligations, or liability that law does not permit limiting. Montana law governs, with good-faith written negotiation first and competent courts in Missoula County, Montana, for disputes not resolved voluntarily. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.

12. Incorporated policies and notices

The Website Ownership & Handoff Terms, Refund Policy, and Cancellation Policy are incorporated by reference. The Privacy Policy describes MX's own personal-data handling. Material changes will not retroactively alter an existing accepted order without agreement. Formal notices and customer requests go to TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com until a verified replacement contact is posted and given to active customers.
