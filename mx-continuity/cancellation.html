Cancellation Policy

MX CONTINUITY | Public customer policy | Effective November 9, 2026

1. Website setup projects

Before launch, a Customer may cancel a setup project by emailing TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com and identifying the business and project. MX will stop avoidable new work after receiving the request, account for completed work and any preapproved third-party expenses, and calculate any remaining refund under the Refund Policy. Cancelling a build does not automatically cancel a separate domain, hosting, or software subscription held by Customer with another provider; Customer must manage those arrangements directly or request available help.

2. Changes to projects and additional work

A Customer may cancel a separately agreed Change Order & Additional Work Authorization by emailing the support contact and identifying the original Project Order and the affected Change Order. MX will stop avoidable additional work and account for the work performed and any approved external expenses under the Refund Policy. Ending a Change Order alone does not terminate the original project, customer-owned assets, or independently authorized monthly care; the Customer may request those cancellations separately.

3. Included 30-day care is not a subscription

Every completed and confirmed site launch includes 30 consecutive days of care following the selected plan's review schedule. The 30 days begin on the confirmed go-live date, not the purchase date. When the included period ends, service care simply stops unless Customer separately opted into monthly billing. MX will not auto-convert included care into a paid plan.

4. Optional monthly care: cancel anytime

Customers who separately enroll in monthly care can cancel at any time, without a cancellation fee, minimum term, or mandatory telephone call. A cancellation request received before the next recurring charge is effective for that upcoming renewal; MX will not intentionally bill for a new period after receiving it. Customer can email TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com with the business name and the words "Cancel MX monthly care." Email cancellation does not require a telephone call or a discussion with a salesperson. When an authenticated online self-service cancellation or payment-processor portal is implemented, MX will publish and offer it as an additional straightforward method; the email route remains valid.

If a charge is captured after a timely cancellation request due to processing delay or error, MX will refund the erroneous renewal. MX will acknowledge a valid cancellation request within two business days; acknowledgement is not a prerequisite for the cancellation to be effective. MX may reasonably verify the requester controls the service but will not impose needless retention interviews or barriers.

5. Effect of cancellation

Care continues through the already-paid term unless the Customer asks for immediate cessation or the parties agree otherwise. No further monthly care charges accrue. Customer keeps its fully paid original custom deliverables and Customer-owned domain, subject to third-party licenses and the Website Ownership & Handoff Terms. The site may stop functioning if Customer does not maintain its domain, hosting, required apps, or other external services. MX will provide available account/access handoff as specified in the Ownership & Handoff Terms. Cancellation of MX care does not itself cancel separate vendor plans.

6. Changes in price or service

MX will not silently move Customer to a higher recurring price. A price change or material scope change requires clear advance notice and the Customer's express agreement before the new price is charged. Customers may cancel instead. If an enrolled card payment fails, MX will notify the Customer and may suspend care after a reasonable opportunity to correct billing; it will not claim ownership of Customer assets or retain their domain for unpaid monthly care.

7. Contact and records

Cancellation notices, confirmation, and related billing questions: TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com. MX will retain a record of cancellation, effective date, final authorized charge, and transfer request. Customers will receive written confirmation of the care end date and any remaining hosting/third-party obligations.

Questions about these terms? Email TheUrbanMatrixLab@outlook.com. Service obligations are confirmed through an accepted written project order.
