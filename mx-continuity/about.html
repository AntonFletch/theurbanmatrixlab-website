About our approach

Thoughtful sites. Dependable care.

MX Continuity is a proposed dedicated website design and maintenance brand. We focus on straightforward, mobile-friendly sites and a practical maintenance schedule, rather than overwhelming customers with technical jargon.

Built to serve a real business

A well-made website should make it easier for customers to understand what you do, contact you and trust your business. Our packages start simple and grow in scope only when you need more pages or functionality.

Care with boundaries

Monthly maintenance has a stated weekly cadence, separate customer authorization and clear exclusions. We do not promise unstaffed 24/7 support or unlimited new features for a fixed monthly fee.
