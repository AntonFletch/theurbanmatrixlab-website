Frequently asked questions

Clear answers, no surprises.

Is the monthly plan included in the startup price?

The first 30 days of care are included after your website goes live. After that, ongoing monthly care is optional. It is not automatically activated or billed by a setup purchase.

How often do you check my website?

Basic and Business include one scheduled checkup per week. Continuity includes scheduled checkups on Mondays and Fridays. Checks include website function, key links, and forms as applicable to your site.

Can I cancel monthly website care?

Yes. Monthly website care can be canceled anytime after you separately enroll. One-time setup charges are not monthly subscriptions.

Does the price include buying my domain?

Domain purchase and paid third-party subscriptions are separate. We can connect a domain you already own, or coordinate a new domain as part of website setup.

Can you build an online store or custom client portal?

Yes, those can be discussed as separately quoted custom projects. The advertised standard packages do not automatically include specialized ecommerce, customer portals, or advanced software integrations.

Do you offer 24/7 monitoring and instant repairs?

Website issues can be reported at any time. Scheduled reviews are performed on the days stated in your package. This does not constitute a guarantee of instant response, nonstop automated monitoring, or uninterrupted uptime.
