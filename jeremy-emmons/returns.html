Returns, Cancellations and Damaged Art

Effective only after final review and publication. This website is a preview, not a payment-taking store.

Before an order

An inquiry is not a purchase. No deposits or payments are taken on this preview site.

Before fulfillment

When real orders are enabled, the artist will confirm the order and provide a clear cancellation process. If a prepaid item cannot be fulfilled as promised, applicable cancellation/refund rights apply.

Damage or incorrect description

Buyers should photograph the packaging and artwork promptly and contact the seller through the contact method shown in their final order confirmation. Work that arrives damaged, is not delivered, or is materially misdescribed will be reviewed for an appropriate remedy, including a return/refund when required by law.

Change of mind

A final voluntary change-of-mind return window and any exclusions for customer-requested framing have not been approved. These must be displayed before accepting real orders. No "all sales final" blanket term is in effect.

Refund method

Where a refund is due, the seller will use the original payment method where practical, subject to applicable payment rules and law. Final refund timing and contact details must be published with live checkout.
