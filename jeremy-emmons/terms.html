Website Terms and Artwork Inquiries

Effective only after final review and publication. This website is a preview, not a payment-taking store.

Status

This is a preview gallery and is not currently accepting orders, reservations or payment. All artwork titles, prices, framing upgrades and availability are proposals until Jeremy Emmons confirms them in writing.

Ownership

The paintings are presented as original works by Jeremy Emmons. Purchase of a physical painting does not transfer copyright or reproduction rights unless a separate signed license states otherwise.

Product details

The photographs depict physical originals. Screen colors can differ from physical colors. Medium, year, dimensions, canvas depth and condition must be confirmed before payment. Frame views are illustrative, and supplier listings are not promises of fit or stock.

Requests, acceptance and payment

Preparing or copying an inquiry does not create a contract or hold an artwork. A sale requires individual confirmation of the work, exact price, any approved frame and costs, fulfillment details, and an accepted payment method from the artist or an expressly authorized seller.

Delivery, cancellations and issues

Approved order confirmations must specify collection/shipping options, expected timing, cancellation and return rights, and remedies for damage, misdescription or nondelivery. See the Delivery and Returns pages. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

Accuracy and contact

A support email, postal contact, shipping scope and business address must be approved and added before real orders go live. This site currently provides no online payment processing.
