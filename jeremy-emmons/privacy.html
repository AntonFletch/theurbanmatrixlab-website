Artwork inquiry privacy notice

Privacy Notice

This website accepts artwork inquiries only. No payment cards, orders or deposits are collected through this website.

What this site does

This website showcases original artwork by Jeremy Emmons and collects requests from people interested in a painting. It does not provide a shopping cart, online checkout, customer account creation or payment collection.

Information collected and why

When you send an inquiry we collect your name, email address, optional phone number, painting number, whether you want a frame, and any message you enter. This is used only to respond, discuss availability and arrangements, and keep a record of your inquiry. We also apply request-rate limiting to protect the form against abuse. Do not send card information or other sensitive details.

Who handles it

Inquiry records are stored in a restricted table using the site's Supabase backend, separately from other UMX business inquiries. The artwork seller and authorized site administrator may access submitted inquiries for follow-up. The site is delivered over HTTPS. Submitted data is not advertised as public and is not sold to advertisers.

Browser storage

The site may use sessionStorage only to remember whether to replay the signature introduction during your browsing session. Inquiry details are transmitted to the secure submission endpoint when you choose Send; the form does not accept credit-card details.

External sites

Frame product links lead to an independent retailer. That site has its own privacy terms and may process your data if you visit.

Retention and requests

Inquiry records are kept while needed to respond, resolve a request, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. To request access to or correction or deletion of inquiry details, use the contact email below. Requests may require verification.

Contact

For site privacy questions and requests, contact the UMX site administrator at theurbanmatrixlab@outlook.com. Artwork sales remain subject to Jeremy's confirmation.
