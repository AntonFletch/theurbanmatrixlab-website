A FEW DETAILS

Questions before collecting?

Are these original paintings?

Each displayed work is represented as one unique physical original. Medium, dimensions, condition and ownership must be verified by Jeremy before payment.

Is the framing included?

No. The original painting is priced separately. Frame upgrades shown are proposed retail additions; exact fit and supplier availability must be checked before accepting an order.

Can I collect in Missoula?

Local pickup may be arranged directly with the seller after an order is approved. No public pickup location or schedule is promised yet.

Does this request hold a painting?

No. A submitted inquiry is not a reservation or purchase. A confirmed invoice and payment arrangement will be provided by the authorized seller when ready.
