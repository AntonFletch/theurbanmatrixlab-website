Storefront policy / draft for approval

Accessibility Statement

Effective only after final review and publication. This website is a preview, not a payment-taking store.

Intent

The gallery aims to support keyboard navigation, descriptive image text, scalable layouts, readable contrast, reduced-motion preferences and alternative frame-selection buttons.

Known limits

Product photographs have not yet been professionally color calibrated, and real-world frame fit requires measurements. Testing across devices, screen readers, and the live hosting environment remains to be completed.

Assistance

An accessible customer contact method must be configured before publishing. If a visitor cannot use a gallery feature, the seller should provide artwork details and alternative purchase assistance on request.

Updates

This statement describes the current preview only and will be updated after full device and assistive-technology review.
