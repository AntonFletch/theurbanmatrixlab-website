Accessibility information

Accessibility Statement

The artist gallery is available for viewing and inquiries. Contact us if a feature is difficult to use.

Intent

The gallery aims to support keyboard navigation, descriptive image text, scalable layouts, readable contrast, reduced-motion preferences and alternative frame-selection buttons.

Known limits

Product photographs have not yet been professionally color calibrated, and real-world frame fit requires measurements. Automated checks do not replace comprehensive testing on physical devices and with screen readers; those checks are not yet confirmed.

Assistance

If you cannot use a gallery feature or the contact form, email theurbanmatrixlab@outlook.com with the painting number. We can respond with artwork details and alternative ways to inquire.

Updates

This statement will be updated as accessibility checks and improvements are completed.
