Local Pickup, Shipping and Framing

Effective only after final review and publication. This website is a preview, not a payment-taking store.

Local collection

Local pickup in Missoula will be available only when individually agreed in the order confirmation. No public pickup address or hours are represented by this preview.

Shipping

Shipping availability, destination, packaging, insurance, costs and delivery timing are not yet configured. Buyers may request a shipping quote; no amount shown is a shipped total.

Framing

Illustrations show a possible finished appearance, not proof a particular supplier frame fits. Exact canvas width, height and depth, supplier inventory and shipping must be checked before a framed order is accepted.

Timing and delay notices

For future online prepaid orders, fulfillment dates must be based on a reasonable basis. If the seller cannot ship by the promised deadline, they must obtain consent to a delay or issue a prompt refund as applicable under the FTC Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule.

Packing and handoff

The operator should photograph condition before packing, use adequate protection for frame corners and painted surfaces, retain carrier receipt and tracking where shipped, and log buyer acceptance at local pickup.
