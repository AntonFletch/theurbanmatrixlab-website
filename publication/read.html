The Story While It Is Still Happening

I don't know how to tell this story in order.

Not because the order has been lost.

Because my life rarely seems to happen that way.

One story begins while another is still happening. A joke interrupts something painful. Something painful disappears behind a joke. A stranger becomes important for twenty minutes and may change the direction of an entire day. A decision that looks enormous afterward can begin with almost no thought at all.

There are photographs from pieces of my life. Not enough of them. A hotel room. An airport. A street. A couch. A job. A business. A person I met. Somewhere I lived. Somewhere I never intended to stay. Somewhere I never expected to be.

The photographs never lied.
They just never explained anything.
Neither did I.

Sometimes an entire system was being built in my head while I looked like a bum walking down the street.

Sometimes I was working. Sometimes I was helping somebody. Sometimes I was building something because I believed it should exist.

And sometimes I did something completely unnecessary because I already knew I was going to laugh when it worked.

You're looking for a motive.

I was having fun.
