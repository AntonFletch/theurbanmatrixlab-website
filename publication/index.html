The Door Is Open

A life in progress does not begin by explaining itself. It begins in the middle.

I do not know how to tell this story in order.

Not because the order has been lost.

Because life rarely seems to happen that way.

One story begins while another is still happening. A joke interrupts something painful. Something painful disappears behind a joke. A stranger becomes important for twenty minutes and may change the direction of an entire day.

The rest continues behind the door.
