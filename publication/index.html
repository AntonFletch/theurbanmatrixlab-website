CURRENT EDITORIAL RULE

The life is his. The book is mine to write. Not mine to invent. Not mine to own. Mine to write.

01

Preserve the words.

Direct quotes, reported memories, observed events, outside dialogue, and narrator interpretation are not interchangeable.

02

Keep open questions open.

When the source is uncertain, the manuscript keeps the uncertainty instead of manufacturing certainty.

03

Keep the ordinary evidence.

The sandwich, the bus, the arcade screen, the job, the room, the photograph, the mistake, the correction — none of it gets discarded just because it is not dramatic.
