The Black Box Recorder

Every correction made the book less elegant and more accurate.

That is the correct trade.

A traditional biography benefits from distance. Years pass. Documents accumulate. People are interviewed. Contradictions can be checked against a timeline.

A live biography has the opposite problem.

It is too close to the source.

STUDY

The correction is not cleanup. It is part of the evidentiary record.

The ordinary evidence stays.

The sandwich. The arcade screen. The gallon of milk. The job note. The bus. The room. The photograph. The joke.

Not every ordinary detail becomes a symbol. Some things remain exactly what they were.

That is what keeps the mythology from swallowing the human being.
