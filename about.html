The Beginning

More Than a Business. A Bigger Idea.

I founded The Urban Matrix Lab LLC in Missoula, Montana, in June 2026 with a vision that went beyond creating a single business.

I wanted to develop practical businesses, useful technology, and organized systems that could solve real problems, create opportunities, and provide lasting value.

What began as business planning grew into something more ambitious: the idea of building independent operations connected by a common standard of responsibility, reliability, and continuous improvement.

The Urban Matrix Lab became the foundation for that vision.

The Mind Behind the Matrix

I See Possibilities Where Others See Problems.

I've always approached building this company by asking questions. What isn't working? What is missing? Why does something have to be complicated? And what would it take to make it better?

An idea alone isn't enough for me. I want to understand how it works, what it costs, what could go wrong, and whether it can actually deliver what it promises.

That mindset has shaped how I approach everything from business planning and customer communication to website development, automated systems, security, and operational oversight.

I believe the smallest details matter because they're often what determines whether something succeeds or fails.

Building Something Real

Learning. Creating. Improving.

As the founder and sole owner of UMX, I take responsibility for the company's direction, its decisions, and the standards behind its work.

Developing this company has meant researching unfamiliar subjects, working through technical challenges, establishing procedures, reviewing legal requirements, and continually improving the systems being built.

I use modern technology, including artificial intelligence, as a resource for research, development, organization, and problem-solving. But technology doesn't replace responsibility. The decisions, direction, and accountability remain mine.

I don't believe in presenting unfinished work as an accomplishment. If something needs improvement, I want it identified, corrected, and tested.

I would rather build something dependable than something that merely looks impressive.

The Vision Continues

Independent Businesses. One Standard.

UMX Technology represents the current technology-focused direction of The Urban Matrix Lab.

Through MX Continuity, I'm developing website services and ongoing digital support systems for businesses. Through MX Visibility, I'm developing services designed to help businesses improve their presence in search and reach the people looking for them.

Each business has its own purpose, identity, responsibilities, and operating structure.

My larger vision remains the same: build practical solutions, create meaningful opportunities, and establish systems capable of growing responsibly over time.
