Privacy Notice

Effective November 11, 2026. This notice covers the separate UMX Field Services Northside pre-launch interest form, operated by The Urban Matrix Lab LLC in Missoula, Montana.

Information we collect

If you submit a form, we collect your name, email, optional phone number, property address and type, requested service, preferred reply method, and whatever details you choose to provide. The inquiry system may record limited technical information such as IP address and browser type to reduce spam and protect the service.

How we use it

To review Northside eligibility, respond to your inquiry, prepare a potential estimate when UMX is operational, maintain an internal contact and property record, and protect the website from abuse. Submitting interest does not create a customer account, service contract, appointment or payment obligation.

Who can access it

Authorized UMX Field Services personnel may access the inquiry in the restricted company operations system. Website infrastructure and database providers process information as needed to operate the service. We do not place Field Services inquiries in MX Continuity or MX Visibility customer records and do not sell inquiry information to advertisers.

Retention and corrections

We keep inquiry information only as reasonably needed for outreach, operations, records and applicable legal obligations. You can request a correction, withdrawal of an inquiry or deletion of information where applicable by emailing us. Some data may be retained where records or security requirements apply.

Site security

The public form sends data over HTTPS to a UMX backend endpoint. Internal records are protected using staff access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not send payment-card numbers, medical details or other sensitive personal information in the form.
