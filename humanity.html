No one gets left behind.

UMX Humanity Center is being built for people who are homeless, in crisis, rebuilding, recovering, trying to work, trying to get housed — and for the person who is not ready yet but may be tomorrow.

Getting some people off the street is not enough if the people with the deepest barriers are simply abandoned. Humanity means building more than one entrance.

The Humanity Pathways

Start with the person who is actually in front of us.

Different people need different interventions. The system is designed to meet the real barrier first, then connect each next step instead of handing someone a list of phone numbers and hoping the rest happens.

01 · STABILIZE

Street & Crisis Connection

Low-barrier connection for people living outside or moving through crisis.

  • Food, hygiene and basic needs connections
  • Phone, ID and document recovery
  • Transportation and appointment navigation
  • Emergency and professional referrals when required
02 · RECOVER

Recovery Support

A pathway for people dealing with substance use, relapse, or repeated crisis.

  • Peer connection
  • Treatment and withdrawal-management navigation
  • Recovery-community support
  • Future recovery-housing pathway
03 · REBUILD

Work & Daily Stability

Turn willingness into momentum through practical work and structure.

  • Daily-labor and employer connections
  • Resume and worker profiles
  • Training and skill development
  • Transportation and work-readiness support
04 · HOUSE

Housing Navigation

Connect people to realistic housing options and the documentation needed to qualify.

  • Property and landlord search
  • Application preparation
  • Move-in assistance coordination
  • Long-term stability planning
05 · CONNECT

Benefits & Essential Systems

Help people reach programs they may already qualify for but cannot navigate alone.

  • Social Security and benefit navigation
  • Medicaid and health-system referrals
  • Food and phone-program connections
  • Identification and records support
06 · CONTINUE

Long-Term Independence

The goal is not permanent dependence on a program. It is a stable life with real choices.

  • Education and advancement
  • Banking and financial basics
  • Permanent employment and housing
  • Follow-up without unnecessary control
A Separate Lane

Not Ready Yet

A relapse, refusal, missed appointment, or failed placement should not automatically become a lifetime label. The person may lose access to a specific placement because of safety or program rules, but they do not become human discarded. Outreach can reconnect when they are ready to try again.

Recovery Is One Part of Humanity

A real path for the people who cannot jump straight from the street into housing or work.

Recovery support belongs inside the broader Humanity system. The objective is to connect people to appropriate professional care, peer support, safe environments, and a next step they can actually sustain.

What Humanity Recovery can become

  • Low-barrier recovery community and drop-in support
  • Certified peer-support connections
  • Transportation to treatment and appointments
  • Partnerships with licensed withdrawal-management and treatment providers
  • Recovery-oriented employment and housing pathways
  • Registered recovery residence when legally and operationally ready

What it will not pretend to be

UMX Humanity will not present itself as a detox unit, medical facility, psychiatric provider, or licensed substance-use treatment program unless and until those services are properly staffed, licensed, and approved.

Help without chaos. Compassion does not require tolerating assault, threats, theft, dealing, harassment, or dangerous conduct. Safety rules protect participants, staff, partners, and the community.
UMX Humanity OS

One coordinated pathway instead of a maze of disconnected systems.

Humanity OS is the operating model behind the Center: track the barrier, the next action, the responsible partner, and what happens if the first plan fails.

01MeetUnderstand the person's actual situation without inventing one.
02StabilizeHandle immediate barriers and urgent safety needs first.
03ConnectLink the right partner, service, employer, landlord, or professional.
04AdvanceMove toward work, recovery, housing, benefits, education, or another defined goal.
05ReturnIf a plan fails, reassess the barrier instead of automatically discarding the person.
The Humanity Standard

Dignity without pretending every behavior is acceptable.

The system is built around human worth, practical accountability, and honest limits.

Meet reality.Start with where someone actually is, not where a program wishes they were.
No false promises.Say what is available, what is not, and what the next real step is.
Boundaries are part of care.Safety rules, consent, privacy, and lawful conduct are not optional.
Failure is information.If a placement fails, learn why. Do not automatically convert one failure into permanent exclusion.
Partners do specialist work.Medical, clinical, legal, housing, and benefit professionals remain responsible for services that require their expertise or authority.
Independence is the destination.The objective is greater control over one's own life, not permanent program dependence.

Current Program Status

UMX Humanity Center is in development. The public operating model, partnerships, compliance structure, intake system, funding plan, and physical-service model are being built in phases.

This website is not an emergency, detox, medical, psychiatric, or crisis-response service. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911. In the United States, call or text 988 for mental-health or suicide crisis support.

The Point

When someone finally says yes, there should be somewhere for that yes to go.

Housing. Work. Recovery. Documents. Benefits. A phone. A ride. A person who answers. The exact next step will differ. The door should still exist.
