Our Services

Practical work. Clear standards. Stronger properties.

UMX provides dependable property services organized around reliability, documentation, respect, and long-term care.

Property Maintenance

Routine property care, upkeep, minor improvements, and practical problem-solving for residential and commercial spaces.

Lawn & Seasonal

Inspections

Junk Removal

Cleaning

Our Story

It’s a movement.

Built by Experience. Driven by Purpose.

UMX exists to build stronger communities, create opportunity, and restore spaces from homes to neighborhoods. We combine practical solutions with a deeper purpose—cleaner spaces, stronger communities, and a better tomorrow.

We saw the gaps. We knew there had to be a better way. So we built a system that connects service, opportunity, training, community support, and long-term progress.

The movement is simple: prove through action that people can build something better together.
